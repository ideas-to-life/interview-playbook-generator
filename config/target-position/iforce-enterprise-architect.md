In this role, you will bridge the gap between complex business strategy and technical execution, ensuring application, data, digital, and cloud ecosystems are scalable, secure, and aligned with overall business growth.
You will lead the continuous evolution of technology landscape, with a heavy emphasis on enterprise cloud transformations (primarily AWS), application modernisation, and data integration across diverse business divisions.
Location: London, UK (Hybrid)
Travel: Occasional international travel (Dubai, UAE).
Key Responsibilities:
Strategic Architectural Vision & Roadmapping: 
Formulate and execute the enterprise-wide target operating model and multi-year technology roadmaps aligned with global corporate strategic objectives.
Analyse complex business requirements, market trends, and emerging technologies to make authoritative technology investment and modernization recommendations.
Rationalise, standardise, and optimise legacy technical estates to reduce complexity, eliminate technical debt, and lower total cost of ownership (TCO).

Lead the architectural design and governance of high-scale cloud platforms, with primary accountability for AWS landing zones, core platform services, multi-cloud strategies, and cloud security patterns.

Governance, Standards & International Stakeholders:
Define enterprise reference architectures, policies, patterns, and principles across business, application, data, and infrastructure domains.
Act as a trusted technical advisor to senior executive stakeholders, translating complex technical concepts into clear business outcomes and aligning regional initiatives with central enterprise standards.
