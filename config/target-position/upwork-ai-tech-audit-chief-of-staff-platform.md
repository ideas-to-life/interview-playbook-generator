Independent Technical Audit — AI Chief-of-Staff Platform

I am looking for a senior software architect / hands-on technical lead to independently assess an existing custom AI chief-of-staff platform.

This is not a development engagement. I want an independent determination of what has actually been built, whether it is technically sound, what is reusable, what is incomplete, and whether another competent team could take it over and finish it efficiently.

The platform is intended to ingest and intelligently use information from email, Google Drive, calendars, meeting transcripts and eventually selected messaging sources. It is designed to act as a personal chief of staff: understand context across those sources, answer questions, identify commitments and open loops, surface important information, and ultimately support workflows/actions with appropriate approval controls.

What I need assessed

Please independently evaluate:

* Overall system architecture and technical design
* GitHub/code quality, maintainability and technical debt
* AI/LLM architecture, retrieval/context approach and data ingestion
* Email, Drive, calendar and other integration architecture
* Security, credentials/secrets handling, permissions and data isolation
* Database/storage/indexing architecture
* Existing tests and acceptance criteria — and whether claimed functionality actually works
* Production/staging architecture and deployment
* Observability, logging and system metrics
* What exists and works today versus what is partial, mocked, fragile or not built
* What should be retained, repaired, replaced or rebuilt
* Whether the current system can realistically be transferred to another engineering team
* Estimated engineering effort, staffing and cost to complete the intended Phase 1

I specifically want the assessor to verify the system rather than rely on the current developer’s documentation or representations.

Access / system size

The selected assessor will receive access to the relevant GitHub repository(s), a sanitized staging environment, architecture/documentation, requirements, acceptance criteria, test results and relevant technical information.

I have requested a final repository and infrastructure inventory from the current developer and will share the exact information shortly with serious candidates.

As a working assumption, candidates can initially assume approximately 25,000–75,000 lines of custom source code, excluding dependencies, generated files, vendored packages and data. This is my estimate and will be replaced with actual repository statistics when available.

I want a concise written assessment that answers:

What do I actually own today? What works? What doesn’t? Is the architecture/code good enough to continue with? What should be rebuilt? Can another team take it over? What would it realistically take in people, time and money to get to the intended product?

Important findings should be tied to actual code, tests, configuration or demonstrated system behavior wherever practical.

I expect this to be a focused assessment, approximately 3–7 business days once access is available, rather than a multi-month consulting project.
