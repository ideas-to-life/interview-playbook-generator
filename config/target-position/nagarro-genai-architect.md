Role Overview

We are seeking an experienced GenAI Architect to define and own the technical architecture for our generative
AI products and platforms, while providing hands-on technical leadership to the team responsible for building
and delivering them. The role will work closely with business and technology stakeholders to shape agentic
architectures that integrate with the GenAI platform and own the end-to-end onboarding journey, from solution
review through to production deployment.
The successful candidate will set the technical direction for LLM-based systems—including retrieval-augmented
generation (RAG), agentic workflows and evaluation—and make pragmatic build-versus-buy decisions. They will
guide solutions from prototype to secure, scalable production, balancing architecture ownership with active team
leadership.

Key Responsibilities
Architecture and Technical Strategy
 Design end-to-end GenAI architectures covering RAG, agentic workflows, prompt and context management,
and model orchestration.
 Work with stakeholders to define agentic architectures that integrate with the organisation’s GenAI platform.
 Evaluate and select foundation models, vector databases, orchestration frameworks, and cloud AI services.
 Define reference architectures, architecture decision records (ADRs), and reusable patterns for the wider
engineering organisation.
 Make informed trade-offs across cost, latency, quality, and risk, including hosted APIs versus self-
hosted/open-weight models and fine-tuning versus prompt engineering.
Platform Onboarding and Deployment
 Own the end-to-end onboarding process, from initial solution review through design, build, and deployment
on the GenAI platform.
 Assess proposed solutions for architectural fit, technical feasibility, and alignment with platform standards;
recommend changes where required.
 Guide onboarding teams through design, implementation, and release, resolving technical blockers and
dependencies.
 Continuously improve onboarding processes, documentation, and reusable patterns based on delivery
experience.
Delivery and Production Readiness
 Take GenAI use cases from proof of concept to production, ensuring robust CI/CD, monitoring, and
observability.
 Build and implement evaluation frameworks covering accuracy, hallucination rates, safety, bias, and
regression testing.
 Own non-functional requirements, including scalability, reliability, security, and cost management (including
token usage and inference spend).
Team Leadership
 Lead, coach, and mentor a team of engineers and data scientists.
 Set priorities, lead technical planning and design reviews, and remove delivery blockers.
