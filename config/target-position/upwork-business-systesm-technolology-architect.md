Summary
We are a growing small business looking for an experienced Business Systems / Technology Architecture Consultant to review our current software environment and recommend a practical future-state setup.

Our business includes ecommerce sales, direct/quote-based retail customers, and business/enterprise accounts with contracts, special pricing, POs and support requirements.

We currently use multiple systems across ecommerce, CRM, accounting, inventory, VoIP/phone, email, messaging, POS and other business functions. Our goal is to simplify the overall technology environment, improve visibility and communication across the business, and make better use of automation without creating unnecessary complexity.

existing platform used - Quickbooks online, crm, shopify, ringcentral voip etc 

Scope

We want the consultant to review our current processes and systems and recommend:

* What should be retained, replaced, simplified or consolidated
* How CRM, ecommerce, inventory, accounting and communications should work together
* Whether an ERP-centered approach should be considered
* Where AI automation, workflow automation and newer tools can practically improve operations
* What should be integrated versus kept separate
* Where custom development is justified
* A practical technology roadmap suitable for a small but growing business

This is initially an advisory and architecture engagement, not an implementation project.
