The opportunity
An established, technology-led business is seeking an experienced Enterprise Architect to support a major technology architecture and documentation project ahead of an upcoming investment funding round.
The business requires someone to bring together its existing technology landscape, mapping its architecture, platforms, infrastructure, security, technical debt and future technology roadmap into one clear and comprehensive picture.
A key part of the assignment will involve preparing the business for technical due diligence, ensuring its technology estate is properly documented and can be confidently presented to investors and senior financial stakeholders.
The successful candidate will need to combine strong technical architecture experience with excellent communication, documentation and presentation skills.
Key responsibilities
Map and document the existing enterprise architecture and technology landscape.
Document the architecture behind two core software products.
Review and document platforms, infrastructure, integrations and technology stacks.
Document software development practices, deployment processes and operational procedures.
Review network architecture and existing security arrangements.
Identify and document technical debt, architectural risks and areas for improvement.
Document future technology plans, including cloud migration and infrastructure development.
Produce technical specifications, architecture diagrams and supporting documentation.
Support technical due diligence ahead of the funding round.
Document security architecture, penetration testing and relevant security measures.
Own and maintain the Software Bill of Materials (SBOM), covering third-party and open-source components, licences, known vulnerabilities and evidence of code ownership.
Prepare clear presentations explaining the technology estate, current capabilities and future roadmap.
Essential experience
Proven experience as an Enterprise Architect or in a comparable senior architecture position.
Strong understanding of software architecture, cloud technology and infrastructure.
Experience documenting complex technology environments.
Ability to produce architecture diagrams, technical specifications and technology roadmaps.
Strong understanding of software development processes and deployment environments.
Excellent communication and presentation skills.
Ability to translate complex technical concepts into clear information for non-technical stakeholders.
Experience working across multiple technology stacks and platforms.
Desirable experience
Experience within complex, technology-led software businesses.
Exposure to real-time data processing, image processing or other technically demanding software environments.
Experience working with cloud-based and server-based platforms.
Previous involvement in technical due diligence, acquisitions or investment funding.
Experience presenting to investors, financial stakeholders or senior leadership teams.
Knowledge of SBOM management, software supply chain security and open-source licensing.
Ideal candidate
The client is looking for someone who can quickly get under the skin of an established technology environment, understand how everything fits together and create a comprehensive, structured picture of the existing architecture.
This is not purely a technical architecture position. The individual must be comfortable producing detailed technical documentation while also being confident enough to present the findings to senior financial stakeholders and investors.
Communication and presentation skills are particularly important.
