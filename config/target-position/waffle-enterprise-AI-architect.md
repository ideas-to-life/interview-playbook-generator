**Statement of Work – Enterprise AI Architect (Contract)** 

Client · Programme · Architecture Workstream · Draft v0.6 · 16 October 2026

| SOW Services |  |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Summary of Consultancy Required and Expected  Outputs** | Contract Enterprise AI Architect to lead the AI Architecture strand of the Client’s transformation programme (“the Programme”): enabling safe, rapid, governed AI use via a proposed three-zone approach (Ideas › Build › Run) and a managed AI gateway.  • Challenge the draft architecture and take it to an agreed, buildable multi-layer design.  • Lead gateway selection; shape identity, egress, grounding and security control design.  • Stand up use-case governance from idea to production. • Hold design authority over the build and chair the architecture forums. • Hand over to a named internal successor. |
| **Engagement Details**  | • **Duration:** 5 months, indicatively 16 Nov 2026 – 16 Apr 2027 (Design Phase plus early Build).  • **Commitment / location:** full time (TBC); Client office or UK remote, on site for key sessions.  • **Rate:** day rate proposed by supplier; subject to Finance and HR approval. |
| **Reporting Line**  | • **Reports to the Head of Architecture,** who signs off deliverables and is the first escalation point.  • Day-to-day delivery coordinated with the Programme delivery lead via weekly Workstream Status. |
| **Key Responsibilities**  | • Review the draft architecture, state what stands and what changes, and evidence every change.  • Detailed design of the multi-layer AI architecture, ending in a build specification.  • Lead AI gateway evaluation and selection (options may include LiteLLM, Kong, Azure APIM).  • Design the request pipeline, environments and egress, identity (Entra ID) and agent identity, supply-chain controls and model provider / residency / isolation (incl. the Client’s customer-facing product).  • Design a permission-aware grounding approach so AI only sees data users are entitled to.  • Set DLP and guardrail policy; own the security control specification and audit evidence.  • Own the use-case register, release gates and approved AI tools / supplier standard.  • Patterns for priority programmes: SDD/SDLC tooling, Routing ML, CS Agent grounding.  • Design authority over the build. |
| **Skills & Experience**  | • **Essential:** enterprise architecture and Zero Trust; Entra ID and conditional access; egress / network controls in production; LLM gateways, routing and supply-chain controls; willing to overturn inherited decisions with evidence.  • **Desirable:** LLM gateway run in production; retrieval over governed data; MCP security; enterprise security reviews; chairing design forums. |
