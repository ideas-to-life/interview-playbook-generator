As discussed, we’re working directly with PwC and two of the lead partners supporting a number of senior AI Architecture hires as they continue to scale their AI capability across both the business and their client base.

What makes this particularly interesting is the focus on genuine agentic AI and AI platform architecture. PwC is investing heavily in moving beyond traditional AI/ML into enterprise-scale agentic AI, looking at how organisations can design, orchestrate, govern and secure AI agents across complex technology environments.

They have developed their own agent OS to help orchestrate and govern agents across different platforms, models and enterprise systems, with a deliberately vendor-agnostic approach. Alongside this, they have a growing ecosystem of AI partnerships across OpenAI, Microsoft, Google Cloud and others, so there is a lot happening in this space.

 We’re currently supporting multiple hires at Director and Senior Manager level. The key requirement is really around the architecture piece — people who have actually designed AI platforms and agentic systems, rather than traditional Cloud, Data or Solution Architects who have simply worked on projects with an AI element.

 The role covers areas such as:

Designing AI platform architectures around business, technical, operational and regulatory requirements
Determining where different approaches make sense, from deterministic automation and AI-assisted workflows through to autonomous and multi-agent systems
Designing scalable, model-independent architectures and making decisions around orchestration, model gateways, vector search, observability, evaluation and agent management
Establishing the right security and governance controls, including guardrails, permissions, tool restrictions and runtime controls
Integrating AI platforms with wider cloud, data, security and enterprise architecture
Considering scalability, resilience, availability, cost and appropriate fallback mechanisms
Producing the architecture artefacts and documentation needed to take these platforms into production
Working closely with engineering and technical teams to make sure the architecture is actually delivered as intended
 
There is also a specialist AI Security Architect variant, with more of a focus on AI threat modelling, agent identity, runtime security and the controls required around agentic systems.

 The main differentiator is that PwC are looking for people who can demonstrate genuine hands-on experience architecting AI platforms and agentic solutions. That is much more important than simply having AI exposure within a broader Cloud or Data Architecture background.

Given what PwC is building and the level of investment going into this area, I think it could be a very interesting opportunity for someone who wants to be close to the forefront of enterprise AI architecture.
