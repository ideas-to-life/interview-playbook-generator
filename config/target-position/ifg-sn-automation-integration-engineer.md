SENIOR AUTOMATION & INTEGRATION ENGINEER
Job specification · 3-month contract · Woking (hybrid)
Impact Food Group is hiring a Senior Automation & Integration Engineer on a three-month contract to set up how
we deliver agentic workflows, advise on the architecture that supports them, and build the first of them.

The role
Title Senior Automation & Integration Engineer
Reports to Head of AI & Automation, Digital team

Interview One technical conversation with the Head of AI & Automation, including a
walk-through of a production workflow you built; one conversation with the Chief Information Officer

About us and why this role exists
We are a schools contract caterer serving primary, secondary and independent schools across England. Our mission
is transforming lives through the power of food. We have built a Snowflake data platform with reporting on top,
integrated our operational systems into it, and now hold a prioritised pipeline of AI and automation use cases across
finance, operations, food, sales and client relationships.
You will work in a small, flat team: the Head of AI & Automation, two AI and automation specialists who build
straightforward workflows and agents, a data engineer on Snowflake and a CRM development team. We work
without rigid role boundaries. When something needs doing and nobody owns it, the person closest to it picks it up,
and that applies at every level, including this one. You will be the first senior engineer dedicated to automation and
integration, so you will set the standard and will need to find the people, access and decisions you need without
waiting to be asked.

What you will deliver
In the first few weeks: establish a repeatable delivery process for agentic workflows (how a use case is scoped,
sized, built, tested, approved, monitored and handed over) and advise on the architecture to support it. We
have considered n8n as a possibility; we want your recommendation on the platform and on how it should be
deployed, secured and operated alongside Snowflake, Microsoft 365 and our operational systems. From there,
start on the use-case pipeline, building the initial priorities we have defined. The work looks like this:
• Complex multi-step workflows that cross systems: for example turning a menu cycle drafted in Excel into
validated, approval-gated updates in our recipe and labelling systems.
• Data-grounded copilots that rank, draft and route work for a team, with a person approving every action
that reaches a customer or moves money.
• Event-driven orchestration across departments at seasonal peaks, such as compiling a handover from a
signed CRM deal and chasing owners for overdue items.
• Bringing workflows already in production to a supportable standard: version control, monitoring, runbooks
and a named owner for each.
The process and architecture you leave behind matter as much as the workflows: the team must be able to
repeat them without you.

Responsibilities
• Define and document the delivery process, then run it: size and sequence the backlog with honest estimates,
dependencies and gates.
• Recommend and stand up the automation architecture: platform deployment, environments, secrets,
monitoring, version control and the integration patterns the team will reuse.
• Design, build and run agentic workflows end to end: trigger, retrieval, model reasoning step, action, human
approval, logging.
• Integrate our operational systems (catering, CRM, customer service, finance, safety and Microsoft 365) through
their APIs and webhooks, reading from and writing back to Snowflake where the data belongs.
• Transfer knowledge to the two AI and automation specialists through structured sessions, pairing and
documentation, so they can build, run and extend workflows after the contract ends.
• Apply our AI Policy, data-handling rules and data-residency requirements in every build: no pupil-identifiable,
staff personal or cardholder data enters an external AI service, and sensitive processing stays inside an approved
boundary.
• Hand over cleanly: by the end of the contract every workflow has a named owner, documentation and a tested
recovery path.

Essential experience
We are looking for recent, hands-on experience, in production within the last two years, and the ability to
adapt it to our environment.
• Demonstrable delivery of enterprise agentic workflows end to end, in production, across several business
systems: you can walk us through one, including how it fails safely.
• Designing and operating the architecture behind them: platform deployment, environments, secrets,
monitoring, version control, security review and change control.
• Hands-on delivery in the last two years on an enterprise orchestration or automation platform, such as n8n or
Power Automate.
• Integrating business systems through REST APIs, webhooks and OAuth, with the retries, idempotency and
rate-limit handling production needs.
• Large language model APIs inside workflows: structured outputs, retrieval over documents, tool calling, and
judging when a rules step beats a model step.
• Sizing and sequencing a backlog with stakeholders, and documenting as you go so a junior engineer can
follow.
• UK GDPR and data residency in practice. We work in schools, so where data is processed and which
services may see it is a hard requirement. You can show how you have designed workflows that keep
personal and sensitive data inside an approved boundary.
• SQL and a cloud data warehouse, ideally Snowflake.
