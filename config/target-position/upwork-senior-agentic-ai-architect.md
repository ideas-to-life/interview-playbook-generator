# Senior Agentic AI Architect — Human + AI Workforce & Multi-Agent Orchestration

We are looking for a **senior Agentic AI Architect / Multi-Agent Systems Architect** who has already designed and implemented AI systems inside a real operating company.

**If you have not already done this for a real company in production, please do not apply.**

This is not a basic chatbot, prompt-engineering, Zapier, Make, or n8n project.

We operate multiple businesses and are building a company-wide AI operating system across Sales, Customer Service, Marketing, Operations, Finance, Construction, and Management.

Our goal is to create a coordinated **human + AI workforce** where AI agents share the right company knowledge, communicate with each other, follow permissions and approval rules, maintain durable memory, and gradually take over repetitive operational work.

We currently have roughly a 50-person organization. We are **not looking to replace people overnight**. We want to progressively redesign the company so:

* AI first assists humans
* AI then performs work under human supervision
* Humans become approvers, exception handlers, managers, and high-value specialists
* Over time, the company can operate effectively with a much smaller, stronger human team supported by AI

## What You Will Do

* Audit our existing AI architecture before changing anything
* Identify what should remain and what needs improvement
* Design a master orchestration layer
* Define agent hierarchy, roles, and authority
* Design shared vs. agent-specific memory
* Design agent-to-agent communication
* Create human approval and escalation gates
* Design maker/checker controls
* Prevent duplicate or conflicting agent actions
* Design durable state, retries, recovery, and idempotency
* Implement observability, logging, audit trails, and performance measurement
* Establish permissions and security boundaries
* Map current human workflows and determine what should be:

  * human-only
  * AI-assisted
  * AI-executed with approval
  * fully automated
* Help us measure when a process is safe to automate further
* Help redesign staffing and responsibilities as AI takes over more work
