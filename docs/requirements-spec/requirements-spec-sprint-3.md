Interview Playbook Generator

Sprint 3 Requirements Specification

Executive Coaching & Knowledge Intelligence

Version: 3.0
Status: Proposed
Priority: High

⸻

Vision

Transform the Interview Playbook Generator from a document generator into an executive interview coaching system.

The output should enable a candidate to prepare for an executive interview in under ten minutes by surfacing the right evidence, narratives, coaching guidance and opportunity-specific positioning from a shared evidence base.

Sprint 3 focuses exclusively on the Interview Playbook Generator.

It does not introduce Resume Writer enhancements or additional career projections.

⸻

Objectives

Sprint 3 shall:

* Improve conversational interview preparation.
* Strengthen executive storytelling.
* Increase evidence quality.
* Improve opportunity-specific coaching.
* Reduce cognitive load before interviews.
* Preserve strict evidence provenance.
* Maintain compatibility with the OKF Bundle.

⸻

Scope

Included

* Interview coaching
* Executive narrative
* Story architecture
* Evidence refinement
* Behavioural profiling
* Executive briefing improvements
* Knowledge model improvements

Excluded

* Resume generation
* Cover letters
* LinkedIn optimisation
* Opportunity analysis engine
* Career Projection Engine

These belong to Sprint 4.

⸻

Design Principles

1. Coaching First

The system shall coach the candidate.

It shall not simply generate polished interview content.

Outputs should resemble guidance from an experienced executive coach.

⸻

2. Conversation over Presentation

Outputs shall optimise for natural conversation.

Avoid producing scripted answers.

Instead generate:

* talking points
* transitions
* reminders
* emphasis
* conversational flow

⸻

3. Evidence First

Every recommendation must be supported by evidence.

The system shall never invent:

* projects
* achievements
* technologies
* leadership claims

⸻

4. Executive Preparation

Preparation should focus on:

* confidence
* clarity
* prioritisation

rather than memorisation.

⸻

Functional Requirements

FR-1 Executive Behaviour Profile

Introduce a new artefact.

Executive Behaviour Profile

It shall describe:

* Leadership Style
* Communication Style
* Decision Style
* Delivery Style
* Stakeholder Style
* Collaboration Style
* Executive Presence

This profile shall be inferred from portfolio evidence.

It shall never contain unsupported personality assumptions.

⸻

FR-2 Conversation Coaching

Replace presentation-oriented guidance with coaching.

Instead of:

I am an Enterprise Architect…

Generate:

* Begin with…
* Mention…
* Pause before…
* Transition using…
* Finish with…

The objective is natural conversation.

⸻

FR-3 Story Architecture

Replace generic story summaries with structured executive stories.

Each story shall contain:

Situation
Challenge
Decision
Actions
Outcome
Business Value
Transition Sentence

The Transition Sentence enables the candidate to move naturally into the next discussion.

⸻

FR-4 Opportunity Alignment

Improve coaching based on opportunity context.

For every major interview theme identify:

* Why it matters
* Evidence supporting it
* How strongly it aligns
* What to emphasise
* What to avoid over-explaining

⸻

FR-5 Conversation Reminders

Add a dedicated section.

Example:

Conversation Reminders
Pause.
Listen carefully.
Answer the question first.
Keep responses concise.
Use business language.
Bring answers back to outcomes.
Avoid over-explaining technology.
Ask thoughtful questions.
Be curious.
Finish confidently.

⸻

FR-6 Signature Achievements

Introduce explicit Signature Achievements.

These differ from career history.

Each should represent:

* major transformation
* leadership moment
* governance achievement
* organisational capability
* AI adoption
* architecture evolution

Maximum of approximately 10.

⸻

FR-7 Story-to-Question Mapping

For each anticipated interview question identify:

* Primary story
* Supporting evidence
* Alternative story

The goal is flexibility.

⸻

FR-8 Evidence Quality

Improve Evidence Cards.

Include:

* confidence
* provenance
* organisational impact
* strategic significance
* recency
* opportunity relevance

Detect duplicate evidence.

⸻

FR-9 Executive Narrative

Improve narrative quality.

Reduce CV-style writing.

Prefer:

conversation

↓

story

↓

evidence

↓

reflection

rather than

career chronology.

⸻

FR-10 Executive Briefing

Enhance the Executive Brief.

Include:

* Executive Positioning
* Top Five Messages
* Three Signature Stories
* Executive Behaviour Profile
* Conversation Strategy
* Risks
* Opportunity Watch-outs
* Questions to Ask
* Final Reminders

⸻

Knowledge Model Enhancements

The OKF Bundle shall include additional semantic relationships.

Examples:

Evidence
↓
Achievement
↓
Theme
↓
Capability
↓
Story
↓
Question
↓
Interview Strategy

This relationship graph improves reasoning while preserving provenance.

⸻

Non-Functional Requirements

NFR-1

Preparation time should remain under ten minutes.

⸻

NFR-2

Outputs should be easily scannable.

Use executive summaries.

Avoid excessive narrative.

⸻

NFR-3

Every recommendation must trace back to evidence.

⸻

NFR-4

Maintain deterministic provenance.

No fabricated experience.

⸻

NFR-5

All opportunity-specific reasoning shall remain clearly separated from evidence extraction.

⸻

Success Criteria

Sprint 3 is complete when:

* Interview preparation feels conversational rather than scripted.
* Stories flow naturally during discussion.
* Executive Behaviour Profile influences coaching.
* Opportunity-specific guidance is clearly visible.
* Evidence is prioritised by relevance.
* Executive Brief becomes the primary preparation artefact.
* The Interview Cheat Sheet can be used independently five minutes before an interview.
* All recommendations remain evidence-backed and traceable.
